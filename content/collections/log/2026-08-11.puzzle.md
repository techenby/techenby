[The Golden Goose](https://magicpuzzlecompany.com/products/the-golden-goose) from the Magic Puzzle Company. These are apparently famous on TikTok, and I got mine at Target. It's Where's Waldo-style, so every little detail is interesting and fun. The twist: once it's done, you rearrange the sections to open up a gap, then open a second bag of pieces to fill it in and reveal a secret.
