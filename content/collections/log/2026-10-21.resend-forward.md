Before this week, the only tech conference I'd been to was Laracon US. [Resend](https://resend.com) Forward was amazing, especially with the high (IMO) percentage of women and AFAB people!

Dave Kiss's eulogy for the software engineer was my favorite talk by far, and it made me excited about programming again in a way I haven't been in a long time. All of the vendor booths were excellent: the Laravel booth had the Drawbot, and the Paper booth had a create-your-own T-shirt. Christina did an amazing job planning and curating this event, including the gourmet Pop-Tarts that she stole from the Taylor Swift wedding.

The day before, I went to the Laravel Founders Panel, and the day after, I finally made it to [Alcatraz](/log/alcatraz).
