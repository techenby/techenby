Alcatraz has been on my to-go-to list for a long time. The last time I was in San Francisco, I didn't get to go because my ex didn't want to. This time, it was amazing to putz around, read everything, go at my own pace, and take as many pictures as I wanted.

The cell block was smaller than I expected, but it still felt like a place I'd never want to be, and it made me feel for the people who were locked up there.

I didn't know about the Indians of All Tribes occupation of Alcatraz from 1969 to 1971, and it was so cool to learn about it and see that the graffiti the protesters added to the welcome sign and water tower has been preserved for over 50 years.
