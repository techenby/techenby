Yesterday I had the privilege of playing at Chicago’s Symphony Center (where CSO plays). I’ve been in band almost continuously since 5th grade, but never thought I would be able to play at such a prestigious venue! Lakeside Pride Symphonic Band and the Atlanta Youth Wind Symphony sounded great and I had such a blast making a dream I didn’t know I had come to life.
