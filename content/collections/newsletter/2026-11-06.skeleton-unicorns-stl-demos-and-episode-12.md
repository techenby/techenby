## Sunny Home Mobile Polish

I polished Sunny Home’s mobile apps this week: fixed an Android launch crash, added a Preferences screen, improved large-text support and contrast, and cleaned up labels, pickers, and the Summary screen. Small fixes, much nicer app.

That work also exposed NativePHP issues, so I sent upstream fixes for accessibility, tab titles, sizing, testing, and Android builds. They’re my first real code contributions to an open source library (not just docs!), which feels kinda huge.

[See the Sunny Home changes](https://github.com/techenby/sunny/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Atechenby+updated%3A2026-11-01..2026-11-06) and my [NativePHP PRs](https://github.com/search?q=org%3ANativePHP+is%3Apr+author%3Atechenby+updated%3A2026-11-01..2026-11-06&type=pullrequests).
