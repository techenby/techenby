## Resend Forward

Before this month, Laracon US was the only tech conference I’d been to. Resend Forward was amazing, especially the number of women and AFAB people there. Dave Kiss’s eulogy for the software engineer made me excited about programming again, and the booths had everything from a drawing robot to gourmet Pop-Tarts stolen from the Taylor Swift wedding.

## Finally visiting Alcatraz

Alcatraz has been on my list for a long time, so I was very happy to finally putz around at my own pace and take way too many photos. I also learned about the Indians of All Tribes occupation from 1969 to 1971 and got to see the protesters’ preserved graffiti on the welcome sign and water tower.
